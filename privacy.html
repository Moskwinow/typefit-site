How the analysis works

Your photos and answers are sent to an AI model that rates visible features on fixed scales. Our own server then calculates the type and season from those ratings with a fixed formula. The model is instructed not to infer ethnicity, race, health, weight or attractiveness, and photos of people who may be under 18 are rejected. The result is a style recommendation generated with AI, not a judgment about you. It is not a decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you (GDPR Art. 22), and you can choose between your two closest types or tell us it doesn’t fit.

With Premium, an image model makes portraits from your front photo that show palette colors, makeup and hairstyles. They are marked in the app as made with AI and carry the provider’s machine-readable mark. Only you can see them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get a copy of your data and information about how we use it (access);
  • have wrong data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format and move it to another service (portability);
  • restrict processing;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, including analytics;
  • withdraw your consent at any time — this doesn’t affect processing before the withdrawal.

In the app: Profile → Privacy & data (delete photos, withdraw consent, turn analytics off, delete account). For anything else, write to support@typefit.app from the address you signed in with. We answer within one month; for complex requests we may extend this by two more months and will tell you why. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive.

You can complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection, Iasonos 1, 1082 Nicosia, Cyprus, commissioner@dataprotection.gov.cy, www.dataprotection.gov.cy, or to the data protection authority where you live or work. We’d appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

If you live in the United States

We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use it for targeted advertising, so no “Do Not Sell or Share” opt-out is needed. California residents have the rights to know, delete and correct their information and not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not collect biometric identifiers such as scans of face geometry: photos are used once for a style rating and deleted, and no template is created or stored. Retention and deletion of photos is described above.

Age

Stylique is for adults 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Photos are stored in private storage that only our server can read. Access to each person’s data is limited to that person by database rules. If a breach puts your data at risk, we notify the Commissioner within 72 hours and tell you directly when the risk to you is high.

Is giving us data required?

Your birth year is needed to use Stylique. Photos, measurements and answers are needed for an analysis; without them we can’t calculate a result. Everything else is optional.
