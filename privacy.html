Who we are

TypeFit is provided by [Company name], [registered address], Cyprus, registration number [number] (“we”). We are the controller of your personal data. Contact: support@typefit.app.

The short version

  • Your photos are used only to work out your style type and color season. They are deleted right after the analysis — at the latest within 24 hours if something goes wrong.
  • We never create or keep face templates, face geometry or any data that could identify you from a photo. We keep only the result and the ratings it is based on (for example “soft lines”, “warm undertone”).
  • We don’t sell or share your personal data for advertising, and we don’t use the advertising ID.
  • You can delete your photos, withdraw your consent and delete your account in the app at any time.

How the analysis works

Your photos and answers are sent to an AI model that rates visible features on fixed scales. Our own server then calculates the type and season from those ratings with a fixed formula. The model is instructed not to infer ethnicity, race, health, weight or attractiveness, and photos of people who may be under 18 are rejected. The result is a style recommendation generated with AI, not a judgment about you. It has no legal or similarly significant effect.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, restrict processing and withdraw consent at any time (this doesn’t affect processing before the withdrawal). In the app: Profile → Privacy & data. For anything else, write to support@typefit.app; we answer within one month. You can also complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus or your local data protection authority.

If you live in the United States

We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t use it for targeted advertising, so no “Do Not Sell or Share” opt-out is needed. California residents have the rights to know, delete and correct their information and not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not collect biometric identifiers such as scans of face geometry: photos are used once for a style rating and deleted, and no template is created or stored. Retention and deletion of photos is described above.

Age

TypeFit is for adults 18 and over. We don’t knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Photos are stored in private storage that only our server can read. Access to each person’s data is limited to that person by database rules.

Changes

We tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
