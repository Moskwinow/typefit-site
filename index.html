How it works

About five minutes from start to your result

  1. Your measurements

    Height and a few measurements in cm or inches. We never ask about your weight.

  2. 14 questions

    Your frame, your outline, your hair, eyes and skin, and which metal looks best on you.

  3. Two or three photos

    A face photo from the front and in profile, and a full-length photo if you like. Daylight, no filters.

  4. Your result

    Your style type with how close the match is, your color season, and a guide to what to wear.

Questions

Good to know

Is Stylique a Kibbe test?

Stylique uses the 13 classic style types based on the Kibbe system and adds a 12-season color analysis on top. Stylique is not affiliated with David Kibbe.

What if the result doesn't feel like me?

You always see how close the match is and your next closest type. If two types are close, you can compare them side by side and pick the one that fits. Premium lets you repeat your analysis any time, for example with photos in better daylight.

What photos do I need?

A face photo from the front and one in profile, taken by a window in daylight, without makeup or filters, with your hair pulled back. A full-length photo is optional and helps read your vertical line.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are processed only after you agree on a separate consent screen. Analysis photos are deleted right after the analysis. If you use try-on portraits, the front photo from your latest analysis is kept for them until you delete it. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Is this body or medical advice?

No. Results are style recommendations made with the help of AI. Try-on portraits are AI illustrations. Treat your result as a starting point to explore, not a rule.

Who is Stylique for?

Adults 18 and over. The app asks for your date of birth when you start.

How do I cancel Premium?

Premium renews automatically each month or year. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period in your App Store or Google Play account settings.
