Delete your Stylique account

In the app

Open Profile → Delete account and data and confirm. Your account is deleted right away.

Without the app

Email support@typefit.app from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We confirm and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. If you signed in with Apple using a hidden email, deleting from the app is the quickest way.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email address and birth year
  • Any photos still stored, your measurements and questionnaire answers
  • Your result, guide, favorites, item checks and feedback
  • Your customer record in our subscription service

Data in encrypted backups is removed as the backups expire, within 30 days. Purchase records that Apple or Google, or tax law, require are kept by them.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a Premium subscription — it is billed by Apple or Google. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
